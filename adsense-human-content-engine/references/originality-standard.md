# Originality Standard

## 1. Independent Construction

Build the article from the user's intent and verified information.

Do not mechanically rewrite a competing article.

---

## 2. Original Structure

Choose the structure that best answers the reader's question.

Do not copy a competitor's:

- heading sequence
- paragraph order
- examples
- distinctive framing
- unusual terminology

---

## 3. Independent Value

Add value through one or more of:

- original explanation
- practical example
- calculation
- decision framework
- trade-off analysis
- edge case
- implementation detail
- limitation
- clarification of conflicting information

---

## 4. Source Use

Sources provide evidence.

They are not templates for sentence construction.

Understand the information first, then explain it independently.

---

## 5. User Experience

If the user provides:

- personal experience
- test results
- photographs
- measurements
- project details
- observations

use those details accurately.

Do not invent missing details.

---

## 6. AI-Detector Principle

Do not optimize for defeating AI detectors.

Do not perform artificial "humanization."

Optimize for:

- independent reasoning
- useful information
- factual accuracy
- natural editorial language
- specific details
- reader value

---

## 7. Originality Failure

Treat the article as failing originality when it:

- follows one source too closely
- merely replaces words with synonyms
- reproduces distinctive source structure
- contains no meaningful independent value

Repair by restructuring and adding independently reasoned value.
